# 1. Problem Definition

## 1.1 Background

Physical activity and exercise are important components of a healthy lifestyle. However, individuals experiencing pain or discomfort may have different needs depending on their personal characteristics, pain-related factors, activity levels, lifestyle, and other circumstances.

A large amount of exercise and wellness information is available online, but much of it is presented as general guidance rather than being tailored to an individual's specific situation.

This creates an opportunity to explore how **Data Science can be used to understand individual patterns and develop more personalized movement and wellness guidance.**

---

## 1.2 Problem Statement

The central problem addressed by Kinetra is:

> **How can Data Science be used to analyze relevant individual and pain-related characteristics and provide personalized movement, exercise, and wellness guidance?**

The project will investigate relationships between user characteristics and pain-related information and explore how these patterns can support personalized recommendations.

---

## 1.3 Existing Gap

General exercise recommendations may not consider differences between individuals.

For example, two people may experience discomfort in a similar body region but differ in:

* Pain intensity
* Pain duration
* Physical activity level
* Exercise habits
* Sleep and recovery
* Lifestyle
* Work-related factors
* Functional limitations
* Other relevant characteristics

Therefore, a recommendation that may be suitable for one individual may not necessarily be appropriate for another.

Kinetra aims to investigate whether a data-driven approach can help address this personalization gap.

---

## 1.4 Proposed Approach

Kinetra proposes a Data Science-based workflow in which relevant user information is analyzed to understand individual profiles and identify meaningful patterns.

The planned high-level approach is:

```text
User Information
       ↓
Pain / Situation Assessment
       ↓
Data Analysis
       ↓
User Profile / Risk Analysis
       ↓
Personalized Guidance
       ↓
Exercise + Precautions + Wellness Guidance
```

As the project progresses, statistical analysis, machine learning, clustering, recommendation techniques, and other Data Science methods will be explored where appropriate.

---

## 1.5 Real-World Relevance

The project addresses a practical problem: people frequently look for exercise and wellness information online and may have difficulty determining which general information is most relevant to their individual circumstances.

A personalized, data-driven approach could potentially help organize relevant information and provide more individualized guidance than a purely generic recommendation.

---

## 1.6 Project Boundary

Kinetra is intended as an **educational Data Science and wellness-support project**.

The project will not attempt to:

* Diagnose medical conditions
* Determine the medical cause of pain
* Prescribe medication
* Replace professional medical advice
* Replace doctors, physiotherapists, or other qualified healthcare professionals
* Make unsupported clinical claims

The system will instead focus on **data analysis, personalization, exercise guidance, and wellness support within clearly defined boundaries.**

---

## 1.7 Problem-to-Data-Science Connection

The problem can be approached through different stages of the Data Science process:

| Stage                 | Application in Kinetra                              |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Problem Understanding | Define the personalization problem                  |
| Data Understanding    | Identify relevant user and pain-related information |
| Data Preparation      | Clean and transform collected data                  |
| Analysis              | Discover patterns and relationships                 |
| Modeling              | Explore appropriate predictive or analytical models |
| Evaluation            | Assess model and recommendation performance         |
| Deployment            | Develop the final user-facing system                |

This connection will be developed progressively as the project advances through the Data Science course.
